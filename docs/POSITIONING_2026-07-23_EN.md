## In one sentence

**NOTARIUS tells the honest story of a piece of information:** where it came
from, what was done to it along the way, where here is native — and where
someone else's has been slipped in, what exactly changed, and how much you should
worry.

It is not storage. Not "encryption." Not "another safe."

---

## How this differs from what already exists

Existing products protect the **PLACE**: where the file is stored and whether it
is locked. A safe, a lock, a password, "access only for our own."

NOTARIUS deals with the **HISTORY**: what happened to this piece of information
over time — and it points a finger at **where exactly it was swapped**.

> They guard WHERE it lies and whether it's LOCKED.
> We tell WHAT happened to it — and find the place of the substitution.

A safe answers the question "was there a break-in or not." NOTARIUS answers the
question "what here is genuine, and what was added — and in which line."

---

## Analogies (so it isn't confused with a safe)

- **An airplane's black box.** It doesn't prevent the incident — but afterward it
  tells precisely what happened and when, step by step.
- **A medical record.** It doesn't heal — but it keeps the true history: what was
  there, what was added, whose entry, where a foreign hand.
- **A chain of custody.** Evidence is not "encrypted" — a continuous honest record
  is kept for it: who held it, when it was handed over, whether it was swapped
  along the way.

NOTARIUS is a black box / a record / a chain of custody **for any piece of
information**: a document, data, a record, media.

## What a person gets out of it

Not a "green check / red cross," but an understandable verdict:

- **CONFIRMED** — the history is whole, the source converges.
- **CONTESTED** — two sources tell different stories; here is where the divergence
  is.
- **FORGERY** — here is the step where the substitution happened; here is what
  exactly changed (value swapped / an invisible one inserted / a piece lost /
  rewritten).

And all of it — in human words, not "error code 0x3F."

## What we do NOT promise (the honest boundary)

- We don't promise that information is **impossible** to touch. We promise that if
  it was touched — it will be **visible and localized**.
- Not "the ultimate truth." We show WHERE they lied — we don't prove that
  everything else is holy ("the trace finds the lie ≠ the trace proves the
  truth").
- The integrity of the wrapper is not the cleanliness of the contents ("sealed ≠
  genuine inside").

This very honesty is the product: not a loud "you are safe," but a sober "here is
what actually happened."
