# What a "reference" is and why everyone reconciles against the same one

In plain words. This is the heart of trust: if everyone has their own reference,
or it can be swapped, the whole check loses its meaning. Let's go through it step
by step.

---

## What a reference is

A reference is **a fingerprint of the original, taken at the moment of its birth
by whoever created the document.**

It is NOT a copy in someone's drawer. There can be many copies, and any of them
can be edited. The reference is one: a cast of the real document, taken once, at
the source, and unchanged ever since.

---

## How it comes to be

When the author creates the document, he **seals it** — presses a button. The
program takes a fingerprint and writes it into a **shared open registry**.

> One document — one fingerprint — one moment in time.

From that second on, another "original" can no longer be made quietly: the
fingerprint of the real one is fixed first.

---

## Where it lies — and why that's the main thing

The reference is not in the author's pocket and not in the counterparty's pocket.
It's in the **shared registry "under glass"**:

- **anyone** can look;
- **no one** can rewrite;
- whoever wrote **first** is the one who stays.

The registry only adds forward. Backdating is impossible: a late record cannot be
passed off as an early one.

---

## How the counterparty works with it

The counterparty reconciles what **arrived** — not against his own copy and not
against the copy the sender sent (that too could have been swapped in transit) —
but **against the fingerprint in the shared registry.**

Matched — it's the original. Didn't match — we'll show exactly where it diverged.

---

## Four "references" — only one is real

| Which reference | What's wrong | Fit for use? |
|---|---|---|
| **One's own** (each side has its own) | everyone has their own "truth" — a dispute with no resolution | ❌ |
| **A shared file that can be edited** | whoever holds the file rewrote it | ❌ |
| **An outsider's** (someone else's slipped in) | reconciling against a forgery = a forgery | ❌ |
| **The author's, sealed at birth, under glass** | one, early, immutable, visible to all | ✅ **this one** |

---

## Why an outsider's reference can't be slipped in

The registry is built so that **the first record wins**. The real author wrote the
fingerprint first — at the moment the document was born. Any later forgery cannot
rewrite that early record: it only "draws itself on the glass" — and
reconciliation exposes it.

So this is **not** "his reference against mine," **not** a shared file someone
quietly edited, and **not** an outsider's slipped in. It is one and the same
fingerprint of the real document, fixed at the source before anyone else.

---

_Bottom line in one line: all sides reconcile not against each other but against
one immutable fingerprint of the original — taken by the author at the moment of
birth and lying under glass, where everyone can see it and no one can edit it._
