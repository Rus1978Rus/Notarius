Semantic provenance · tamper-localization

Semantic provenance for documents. It doesn't store your files — it catches the lie inside them. It shows WHERE and WHAT changed: a swapped amount, a hidden character, a look-alike domain, whose link in the chain broke — even a skipped step. It locates the lie; it does not judge intent.
