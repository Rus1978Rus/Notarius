## Checked: INTERESTING — and an important skeptic's lesson

**Decay capsule (radioisotope TRNG)** — verdict INTERESTING, but the judge
exposed a trap applicable to MANY "physical" ideas:
> "A radioisotope solves the GENERATION of randomness, not UNCOPYABLE
> STORAGE. Once the decay stream is turned into bits for a signature, it's
> ordinary data — the thief copies the frozen bits and walks away quietly.
> Anything stable enough to be a key is stable enough to be copied."

**This is the master filter for the whole problem** (remember it for the
synthesis): a physical process is uncopyable only while it is CONSUMED in
real time on every signature. If it's "frozen into a number," it's data
again, and the protection reduces to an ordinary HSM. True uncopyability
requires the physical carrier to be NECESSARY at the moment of EVERY use.
