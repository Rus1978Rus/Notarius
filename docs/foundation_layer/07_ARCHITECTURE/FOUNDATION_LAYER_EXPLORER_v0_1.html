FO-004 · CORE OPERATIONAL
REVIEW ≠ VALIDATION
Conveyor constitution · Operational rule
CORE
REVIEW ≠ VALIDATION
CONVEYOR_COMPLETED ≠ DOCUMENT_VALIDATED
PROCESS_EVIDENCE = PROOF
Observation
Passing through the conveyor creates process evidence, but is not proof of the object's validity. Four models can reach consensus — and all four can be wrong in the same direction. A quorum does not replace independent verification.
Use in the conveyor
Used in every run. The basis for AUTHOR_SLOT — the final decision always rests with the author. INTEGRATOR = CONSENSUS_ASSEMBLER, not JUDGE.
Cases
Running the card `,` through 4 models
CONFIRMING
CONVEYOR_NORMAL_RESULT. All models returned ACCEPT_WITH_PATCHES. The card got status WORKINGLY_CLOSED — not VALIDATED.
SOURCE: MSL_MIP_CONVEYOR_RUNS_AUDIT_v0_1
Boundary
FO-004 does not deny the value of review. It clarifies: review creates PROCESS_EVIDENCE, not PROOF. The difference matters when making final decisions.
