MAIN INSIGHT
============================================================

MSL/MIP does not copy law.
MSL/MIP independently rediscovered a structural pattern
that the law-enforcement system found centuries ago.

LOW_CONFIDENCE_SIGNAL
→ STRUCTURED_PROCESSING
→ POSITION_FORMATION
→ DECISION

This is a general pattern for raising confidence.
It works in law, forensics, AML, and MSL/MIP.
