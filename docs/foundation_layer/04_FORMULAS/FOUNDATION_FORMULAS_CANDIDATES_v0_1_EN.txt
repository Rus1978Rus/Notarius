============================================================
FF-001
============================================================

FORMULA:
CONFIDENCE_WITHIN_SYSTEM != CORRESPONDENCE_WITH_REALITY

SOURCE: Claude review / TRIAD-EXT-001
CONTEXT: Even a flawlessly functioning procedure can
arrive at a wrong conclusion.
Examples: phlogiston, the ether, eugenics.

SIGNIFICANCE:
A fundamental limit of any conveyor.
A procedure raises internal confidence —
but does not guarantee external validity.

STATUS: HIGH_INTEREST / FOUNDATION_CANDIDATE

============================================================
FF-002
============================================================

FORMULA:
PROCESS_EVIDENCE_ONLY != VALIDATION

SOURCE: Grok review / TRIAD-EXT-001
CONTEXT: A process log shows that the process was carried out
but does not prove that the conclusion is correct.

SIGNIFICANCE:
A direct extension of FO-004 (REVIEW != VALIDATION).
Adds: even a complete log ≠ validation.

STATUS: HIGH_INTEREST / CONVEYOR_CORE_CANDIDATE

============================================================
FF-003
============================================================

FORMULA:
MULTI_MODEL_REVIEW != ADVERSARIAL_PROCESS

SOURCE: Claude review / TRIAD-EXT-001
CONTEXT: Several models do not form a genuine system
of mutual challenge. Models are motivated to give a helpful
answer — not to hunt for weak spots.

SIGNIFICANCE:
Points to a structural gap in the conveyor.
Controlled Provocation = a partial closing of it.

STATUS: IMPORTANT_GAP / ARCHITECTURE_NOTE

============================================================
FF-004
============================================================

FORMULA:
PROCEDURE != HIGHER_CONFIDENCE_GUARANTEED
BAD_PROCEDURE_CAN_LOWER_CONFIDENCE

SOURCE: Claude review / TRIAD-EXT-001
CONTEXT: A poorly designed procedure can
systematically lower confidence.
Cargo cult, security theater, show trials.

SIGNIFICANCE:
A patch to the formula LOW_CONFIDENCE → PROCEDURE → HIGHER_CONFIDENCE.
A procedure is necessary but not sufficient.

STATUS: MANDATORY_PATCH / CORE_GUARD

RECURRENCE != VALIDITY
